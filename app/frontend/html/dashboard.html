📖 Fault Type Reference
⚙️ Ball Faults

Defects on the rolling element surface. Detected by characteristic Ball Defect Frequency (BDF). Three severity levels: 0.007″, 0.014″, 0.021″ diameter.

🔄 Inner Race Faults

Defects on the inner race surface. Related to shaft misalignment. Detected by Ball Pass Frequency Inner race (BPFI).

🔁 Outer Race Faults

Defects on the stationary outer race. Often from contamination or overloading. Detected by Ball Pass Frequency Outer race (BPFO).

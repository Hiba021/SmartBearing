1. Global Explanability

Feature Importance (Random Forest)

When the Random Forest model is trained, it tracks how much each physical feature contributes to reducing uncertainty (Gini Impurity) across its 200 decision trees. Features with higher importance scores are the most critical for accurately diagnosing bearing faults.

Key Takeaway

RMS and Kurtosis dominate the decision-making process. This perfectly aligns with bearing physics: RMS measures the overall vibrational energy (severity of damage), while Kurtosis detects the sharp, impulsive shocks characteristic of localized cracks.

2. Decision Confidence

Information Theory: Shannon Entropy

Most machine learning projects simply look at the highest probability output. However, by applying Shannon Entropy from Information Theory, we can mathematically quantify the uncertainty across the entire probability distribution.

H = - ∑ pi · log2(pi)
